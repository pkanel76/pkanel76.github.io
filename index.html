TRACE · Greece

More than one way to move a parcel

TRACE is about multimodality in logistics operations. Three Greek pilots tested long-haul transport of roll cages by train instead of truck, the use of other available vehicles to stay resilient during unexpected events, and an autonomous-vehicle delivery on the NTUA campus, ACS’s first.

URBANE · Thessaloniki

Planning a locker network with data

In the Thessaloniki Living Lab, delivery data and a city digital twin supported the design and roll-out of an optimised ACS parcel locker network, a base for future multi-operator collaboration.
